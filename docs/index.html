Research Paper

Publication details and methodology overview

Geometry-Aware and Calibrated Uncertainty Learning for Designing Indium Oxide-Based Alternative Sputtering Targets

This paper presents a physics-guided machine learning approach for predicting and optimizing the resistivity of In–Ga–Sn–Zn oxide sputtering targets. The methodology combines domain knowledge through physics descriptors with modern ML techniques for small-data scenarios.

Authors:
